# V1.09 USB 30/30 power bench

Owner requested alternating 30 s awake and 30 s off. This means ESP32 awake with idle/yielding loop (not CPU stress) and actual timer deep sleep, not disconnecting the USB supply. Runs repeatedly until reset/reflash or power removal. Boot monotonic microsecond deadline 30,000,000 us; timer 30,000,000 us. Both radios left uninitialised, with controller/mode checks before sleep. No external GPIO driven, display/sensors/filesystem not initialised. RTC retains cycle counter. Serial reports every 5 s while awake, USB CDC may disappear and early logs may be missed. No OSA session needed; do not start B1/B2 collection for this isolated test. The charger and LED remain powered by USB in both phases; no board hardware modifications. Timer accuracy and actual current must be measured, not assumed. This is not production firmware or the 300 s parked cadence. V1.08 B2 source remains in repo; restore tested V1.08 commit for B2 with OSA V1.41. V1.09 compile/upload and physical measurement pending.

Record supply voltage, settled awake current, settled deep-sleep current, LED fluctuation and observed sleep/wake intervals. Compare before/after any independently verified charger modification, using same meter/supply. Bare USB current is not whole 12 V system current. If unexpected radio state or timer setup fails, remain awake with error; physical reset/reflash required. To recover USB during sleep use BOOT+RESET if needed.
